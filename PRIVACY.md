# CalSci Privacy Policy

> The published version of this policy is at **https://calsci.gauravt9431.workers.dev/privacy**. The page source is `public/privacy.html`; keep the two in sync.

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

CalSci : Scientific Calculator ("CalSci", "the app") is published by Gaurav Tiwari. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Summary

CalSci does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device

These stay on your device, in the app's private storage:

- your calculation history
- the variables and functions you define
- the data lists you enter
- your settings
- the last downloaded currency exchange rates

The app never uploads them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

## Network access

The app makes one kind of network request: it downloads currency exchange reference rates from the Frankfurter API (`api.frankfurter.dev`). It does this at most once a day, and only while the device is online. The request contains no personal information. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service. See the [Frankfurter website](https://frankfurter.dev) for how it handles requests.

All calculations run entirely on your device, including the symbolic math engine.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to download currency exchange rates.
- **Vibrate:** for optional haptic feedback when you press keys. You can turn this off in Settings.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
