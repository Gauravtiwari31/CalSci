Privacy policy.

This policy covers CalSci : Scientific Calculator ("CalSci"), on Android and on the web, published by Gaurav Tiwari.

In short

CalSci collects no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Data stored on your device

These stay on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your calculation history
  • the variables and functions you define
  • the data lists you enter
  • your settings
  • the last downloaded currency exchange rates

They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Network access

CalSci downloads currency exchange reference rates from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.dev), at most once a day and only while you're online. The request contains no personal information. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service.

On the web, the app and its math engine are downloaded from this website and then cached for offline use. All calculations, including symbolic math, run entirely on your device.

Android permissions

  • Internet: to download currency exchange rates.
  • Vibrate: for optional haptic feedback on key presses. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

CalSci is a general-purpose calculator. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
